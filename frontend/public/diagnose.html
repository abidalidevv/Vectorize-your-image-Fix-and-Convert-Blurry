🔬 Edge Defringe Choke & Color Decontamination Architecture

To eliminate halo fringing and residual background borders at alpha boundaries, VectorForge AI applies physical alpha unmixing:

true_fg = np.clip((observed_bgr - bg_color * (1.0 - alpha)) / alpha, 0, 255)
1. Morphological Halo Choke:

Erodes the alpha mask with an elliptical structuring element (cv2.getStructuringElement(cv2.MORPH_ELLIPSE, (k, k))) to physically pull borders inside before blending.

2. Physical Alpha Unmixing:

Subtracts the background color tint leaked into semi-transparent pixels, restoring true pristine foreground RGB.

3. Gaussian Feathering:

Applies subtle sub-pixel Gaussian smoothing to the final alpha channel for silky-smooth vector boundary integration.
